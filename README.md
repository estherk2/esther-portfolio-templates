# About me
Hi, I'm Esther! I'm a first-year student in the Master of Information Systems Management (MISM) program at Carnegie Mellon University. I'm originally from Korea, and before starting my master's degree, I worked as an AI Strategy Manager at a Vision AI company.

In my previous role, I frequently analyzed market trends and customer needs to develop business strategies. This experience sparked my interest in taking Telling Stories with Data, as I wanted to learn how to turn data into meaningful insights and compelling stories.

Through this course, I hope to deepen my ability to interpret data, ask thoughtful follow-up questions, and uncover insights that go beyond the surface. I'm excited to explore how data can tell stories and support better decision-making!
